I am a researcher and engineer with a strong foundation in **Machine Learning**, **Trustworthy AI**, **ML for Security**, and **Computer Vision**. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh in 2024. I have 2+ years of work experience working as a System Engineer.

## Research Interests

* Machine Learning & Deep Learning
* ML for Security & Adversarial Robustness
* Trustworthy AI & Shortcut Learning
* Computer Vision
