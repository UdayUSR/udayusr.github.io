I am a researcher and engineer with a strong foundation in **Machine Learning**, **Trustworthy AI**, **ML for Security**, and **Computer Vision**. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from [Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)](https://www.kuet.ac.bd/), Bangladesh. Currently, I work as a System Engineer at E-Zone Limited, where I manage and maintain 2,600+ carrier-grade datacom nodes for Robi Axiata across Bangladesh.

## Research Interests

* **ML for Security & Adversarial Robustness:** Defending large language model (LLM) pipelines against adversarial prompt injection attacks, threat modeling, and forensic network intrusion detection.
* **Trustworthy AI & Shortcut Learning:** Diagnosing shortcut learning, spurious feature reliance, and data leakage in deep neural networks to ensure robust out-of-distribution generalization.
* **Computer Vision & Visual Forensics:** Frequency-domain analysis for synthetic / AI-generated image detection, and demographic diversity adaptation in medical dermoscopic segmentation.
