## Abstract
Hair occlusion in dermoscopy images is a major impediment to automated skin lesion segmentation and melanoma screening. For individuals with brown skin tones, the lack of representative training datasets leads to significant performance degradation. This study presents a diversity-aware deep learning framework tailored for hair segmentation on brown skin tones. By augmenting and adapting existing dermoscopic data, we trained and comprehensively benchmarked multiple semantic segmentation architectures—including U-Net, Attention U-Net, Nested U-Net (U-Net++), LinkNet, SegNet, and ENet. Our results demonstrate that targeted domain adaptation yields significantly cleaner hair masks and preserves delicate boundary information necessary for accurate downstream lesion classification.
