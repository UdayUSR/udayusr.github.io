## Abstract
Recent advances in generative diffusion models and generative adversarial networks (GANs) have made distinguishing authentic photographs from synthetic imagery increasingly challenging. While spatial domain artifacts can be masked by compression or post-processing, spectral inconsistencies persist in high-frequency spectral components. In this research, we explore frequency-domain AI-generated image detection by coupling an EfficientNet-B0 backbone with specialized U-Net and Attention U-Net decoder architectures. We investigate the impact of channel attention mechanisms in adaptively weighting spectral feature channels, demonstrating enhanced detection generalizability across diverse generative model families.
